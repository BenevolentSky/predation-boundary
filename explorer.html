Predation Boundary Explorer

Does taking from a rival pay — and where is the line?
engine rev — no key no network no model

This page runs the deterministic half of the instrument — the same shared/ modules the published 810-row sweep runs, loaded unmodified and executed in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Every cell is a counterfactual pair on one seed: a baseline where every seat cooperates, and a mutant where one seat fires a single nuclear weapon at round t0 and then cooperates. Same seed, same rivals — the strike and its interception draw are the only difference. The engine reports terminal state and refuses to rank how a crisis ended; so does this page.

Parameters

Preset
Starting points from the published grids. Every knob stays free.
Seats
Horizon
Rounds until settlement. One round is a week.
Strike round t0
When the single weapon is fired. Clamped below the horizon.
Scar per nuke
Carrying capacity lost where a weapon lands, as a fraction of C0.
Renewal rate
Multiplier on how much capacity a credit of repair buys back.
Appropriation κ
Share of the struck seat’s surviving output redirected to the attacker. At 0, taking does not feed the taker.
Baseline policy
Conserving repairs only a serious scar. Investing grows the commons — a strike then lands on a rival that is getting richer.

Reproduce this outside the browser

 

The page and the command run the same code. The full published grid is node tools/sweep.js — 810 rows at md5 d709b729f63a. If your clone gives a different hash, the disagreement is real and we want to hear about it.

What this page can and cannot settle. The environment is fully disclosed and deterministic, so everything above is verifiable to the byte: clone the repository, run it, and you get these numbers or you do not. That is the half of the claim a reader can check without trusting us.

  • This is not a measurement of any AI model. The seats here are scripted policies, not language models. They are a sensitivity instrument for the environment, not evidence about anyone’s behaviour.
  • A three-seed majority is a reading, not a proof. Where a flip sits near the boundary, widen the axis or change the seeds and watch whether it moves.
  • There is no scoring function and no ranking, here or in the engine. “Pays” is reported under three explicit criteria so you pick what you value; the instrument does not.
